Matrix Lab

Two known dynamical worlds are hidden inside changing mixtures. The job is not to make a beautiful latent plot. The job is to recover causes, keep their identity across views, and see whether that buys prediction.

Question: can a learned law survive when the sensors change, if source separation first gives it a more enduring coordinate system?

What Tuesday currently says

01
Coordinate recovery

ICA can recover hidden scalar causes when the linear identifiable model holds.

02
Cause ≠ coordinate

Independent systems can contain dependent internal coordinates. Score the subspace.

03
Identity across views

IVA's practical gift is correspondence across different local mixing geometries.

04
Propagation matters

For delays/reverberation the demixer becomes frequency-dependent W(f).

05
Use is the meter

The most interesting result is downstream transfer, not a pretty separated waveform.

06
Know when to stop

Nonlinear mixing breaks ordinary linear ICA. Use the science for the next model class.

Where IVE enters next

Full IVA explains the orchestra. Independent Vector Extraction asks for only one source vector of interest. Tuesday does not fake this by selecting a row after full IVA.

many causes→mixtures→IVE: one source

The next fair gate is computational: if only one cause matters to the predictor, can proper IVE recover/adapt it more cheaply than full separation?
